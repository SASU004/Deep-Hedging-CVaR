# QuantForge

> AI-Powered Deep Hedging Research Platform

QuantForge is a web platform built alongside the Deep-Hedging-CVaR research project. It provides a structured interface for exploring deep hedging concepts, strategy comparison workflows, stress-testing scenarios, and quantitative finance research.

## Live Demo

Website: https://hedgelab.vercel.app

Research Repository: https://github.com/SASU004/Deep-Hedging-CVaR

---

## Overview

The platform was developed to present and organize research related to deep hedging and CVaR-based risk management through an accessible web interface.

Current modules include:

* Learning resources
* Strategy comparison workflows
* Stress-testing concepts
* Research-oriented dashboards
* Quantitative finance visualizations

---

## Features

### Learn

Introduces concepts related to deep hedging, option risk management, and quantitative finance.

### Lab

Dedicated area for research exploration and future experimentation workflows.

### Strategies

Comparison-oriented interface for studying different hedging approaches.

### Stress Testing

Visualization and analysis of market stress scenarios and risk behavior.

### Results

Dashboard for presenting research findings, metrics, and performance summaries.
